Every tab, its own history

dude records each tab as a navigation tree: the branch you started after going back, the tabs a page opened, a screenshot of every page and its text. Browse it, search it, replay it. It's a history of your own, next to the browser's.

Branches, not a list

Follow a link, go back, follow another: the browser forgets the first path, dude keeps both as branches of one tree.

Tabs know their parent

A tab opened from a link stays linked to the page it came from, both ways, so the trail continues across tabs.

Pictures and words

A screenshot and the readable text of every page, plus the link you clicked and what you searched for.

Where did I come from?

The path to this page, one click away

The toolbar popup lists the pages that led here, with the link text you followed. The Firefox sidebar or Chrome side panel shows the active tab's tree live as you browse.

Semantic back (Alt+Shift+Up) takes you to the page you actually came from, even when Back would go somewhere else.

Three views of one tab

Tree, moves and network

Tree shows where each page came from. + Moves and Network also draw how you moved between pages, back moves included.

Straight runs fold into one card, and long pauses show between visits. Double-click a card to open the page; Open with path reopens it with the pages before it, so Back works again.

Find it again

Search everything you read

Titles, addresses, page text, search terms and link texts, filtered by time, site and tab family. Each hit shows the pages that led to it, across tabs too.

In the address bar, type h and a space, then your words.

Replay a day

Playback

Step through one tab, a tab with the tabs it opened, or the whole day, with a screenshot at every step. Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys step, and idle time is shortened.

At a glance

The wall

Every screenshot, newest first. Sometimes you remember what a page looked like better than what it was called.

Your history stays yours

dude has no server and no account. What it records lives in your browser's own storage, and you decide what goes in.

Local only. Everything is kept in the extension's IndexedDB. dude sends nothing anywhere.

No screenshots where they don't belong: never on banking, payment or password-manager sites, and never while a password field is visible.

Pause and exclude. Pause one tab or all of them, and list sites that are only recorded as anonymous placeholders.

Delete and take it with you. Delete by page, session, site, time range or everything. Export and import as a zip.

Install

Prebuilt packages come with every release. dude isn't in the browsers' add-on stores, so it takes a few clicks.

Firefox

  1. Download dude-version.xpi. Mozilla signed it as an unlisted add-on.
  2. Open about:addons, click ⚙ and choose Install Add-on From File.
  3. Pick the file. dude updates itself from then on.
Download for Firefox

Chrome

  1. Download dude-version-chrome.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. Keep the folder: Chrome loads dude from there, and doesn't update it.
